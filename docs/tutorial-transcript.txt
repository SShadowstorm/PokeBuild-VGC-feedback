PokeBuild VGC 0.2.1 — animated quick-start tutorial
English synthetic narration generated locally. Controls are illustrated.

Build. Test. Refine.
Welcome to PokeBuild VGC, a Windows team builder for Pokémon Champions. This animated guide shows how to build a team, explore Mega forms, and check your matchups.

01 · Install & open
On the GitHub release page, download PokeBuild VGC Setup, version zero point two point one. Run the installer and open PokeBuild from its shortcut.
You need 64-bit Windows ten or eleven. The installer is unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning. Only use the trusted project download.

02 · Pick your six
Open Team builder and select a team slot. Use Browse Pokédex to search by name, filter by type, or sort by Speed and base stat total.
Choose a Pokémon. The editable reference set gives you a starting point, and you can change every choice afterward.

03 · Make the set yours
Choose the held item, ability, nature, and four moves. Adjust the Stat Points to match the build you want to test.
Champions uses a maximum of sixty-six Stat Points in total and thirty-two in any one stat. These are not Scarlet and Violet effort values.

04 · Explore Mega Evolution
For example, give Barbaracle its matching stone, Barbaracite. Calculate after Mega Evolution activates the Mega form for stats, typing, and damage checks.
The Mega panel compares the base and Mega forms. Only one Pokémon can Mega Evolve per battle. All eighty-one Mega forms have their own images.

05 · Check a matchup
Use Browse all opponents to choose a Pokémon. Edit its item, ability, nature, points, and moves to test a specific opponent set.
Check incoming damage and outgoing damage. The app scans the selected attacking moves and shows the strongest one first. Set weather, terrain, and battle stages yourself.

06 · Compare & adjust
Usual matchups offers website-sourced opponent profiles. Pick one to load it into the calculator, then compare it against your current build.
Use Matchup matrix to compare your team against saved opponents. These checks describe individual attacks, and the usual matchup percentage is not a battle win rate.

07 · Save & share a team
Your workspace saves on your own device. Use Import team to paste a Showdown Champions team, or Export to download your team as a text file.
Share that file with another tester. Teams are not uploaded automatically, and calculation data and Pokémon images are bundled for offline use.

08 · Help improve PokeBuild
Use Bugs and suggestions to report a problem or suggest an improvement. Include what you clicked, what you expected, and what actually happened.
Review and submit the report on GitHub. Reports are public and require an account. Without GitHub, save the feedback as text and send it to the developer.
